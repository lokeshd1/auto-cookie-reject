# Privacy Policy for Auto Cookie Reject

**Last Updated:** May 29, 2026

## Overview

Auto Cookie Reject is a browser extension designed to automatically reject cookie consent popups. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any data.**

Auto Cookie Reject:
- Does NOT collect personal information
- Does NOT collect browsing history
- Does NOT collect any user data
- Does NOT transmit any data to external servers
- Does NOT use analytics or tracking

## Data Storage

The extension stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Enabled/disabled state | Remember your preference | Chrome sync storage |
| Disabled domains list | Per-site settings | Chrome sync storage |
| Statistics (banner count) | Show in popup | Chrome sync storage |

This data:
- Never leaves your browser
- Is synced only via your Chrome account (if sync is enabled)
- Can be deleted by uninstalling the extension

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|------------|----------------|
| `storage` | Save your settings locally |
| `activeTab` | Show protection status for current tab |
| `<all_urls>` | Detect and reject cookie banners on any website |

## Third-Party Services

This extension does NOT use any third-party services, APIs, or analytics.

## Data Sharing

We do NOT share any data because we do NOT collect any data.

## Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
